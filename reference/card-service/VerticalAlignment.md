# VerticalAlignment

An enum that sets the vertical alignment of widgets in a column.

An enum that sets the vertical alignment of widgets in a column. Available for Google Chat apps and Google Workspace add-ons.

To call an enum, you call its parent class, name, and property. For example, `CardService.VerticalAlignment.TOP`.

## Properties

### CENTER
Vertically places the content at the center of the container. Default.

### TOP
Vertically places the content at the top of the container.

### BOTTOM
Vertically places the content at the bottom of the container.
