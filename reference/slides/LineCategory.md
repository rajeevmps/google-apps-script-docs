# LineCategory

The line category.

The line category. The exact `LineType` created is determined based on the category and how it's routed to connect to other page elements.

To access these values, use `SlidesApp.LineCategory.PROPERTY_NAME`.

## Values

- `UNSUPPORTED` — A line category that is not supported.
- `STRAIGHT` — Straight connectors, including straight connector 1.
- `BENT` — Bent connectors, including bent connector 2 to 5.
- `CURVED` — Curved connectors, including curved connector 2 to 5.
