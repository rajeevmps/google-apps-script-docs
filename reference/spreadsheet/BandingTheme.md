# BandingTheme

An enumeration of banding themes.

An enumeration of banding themes. Each theme consists of several complementary colors that are
applied to different cells based on the banding settings.

To call an enum, you call its parent class, name, and property. For example, SpreadsheetApp.BandingTheme.LIGHT_GREY .
